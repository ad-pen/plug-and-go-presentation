04 / 18 The Solution
An Airbnb for EV chargers

A dual-sided marketplace built for two unmet needs.

For EV Drivers

Predictable, bookable charging

Reserve a nearby private charger before you arrive. Real-time availability, in-app payment, turn-by-turn navigation, vehicle-compatible filtering. Range anxiety becomes a planning problem instead of a panic.

For Charger Hosts

Passive income from idle assets

Turn an existing home Level-2 charger into a recurring revenue stream. Set your own price, control availability, get protected by verification and insurance. ~4,600 JD/year at 3 sessions/day.

PLUG & GO 11 — Kano Model
12 / 18 Risk & Safety
A framework borrowed from Airbnb & Uber

Trust is built before the first session, not after the first incident.

→ Verification & Insurance

  • Hosts — Electrical installation verified by licensed electrician before listing
  • Drivers — Vehicle & license verified at signup
  • Insurance — Liability covered through third-party partner or built into transaction fee
  • Mandatory onboarding checklist — Smoke detector, dry-powder extinguisher, clear charger area

→ Shared Liability Model

  • Host — Liable for known faulty equipment or wiring
  • Driver — Liable for incompatible chargers or tampering
  • Platform — Liable for failure to enforce safety standards
  • 24/7 emergency line — Active during every session for both sides
